# Game Glance 3.4.1

Fixes for the games row, the way into a game's page and play time for games from Lutris. Update from Quick Access → Game Glance → Updates.

- **Your games in the order Steam's Home shows them.** Games you played or installed on this device come first. Games
  you played only on another PC or handheld follow later in the row (from the 16th place), as on Steam's Home. A game
  you played here that Steam's servers have not seen yet shows its last played time instead of "Added".
- **Changed your mind? Press B.** Right after you pick a game, B now takes you straight back to Home with Steam's back
  sound and the same fade as coming back from a game page, and A works again at once. Once the game page is up, Steam
  handles B as always.
- **Play time for games from Lutris.** Games you added to Steam from Lutris show the play time Lutris counted, not
  Steam's count of how long the shortcut ran, which can be much higher (Red Dead Redemption 2: 200+ hours in Steam for
  about 40 in Lutris).
- **No missed first press.** After coming back from a game page with B, the first press of the D-pad always moves the
  selection.
- **No flicker at the top when opening a game.** With Game Glance's status bar turned off, or with Steam's own game
  page, Steam's top bar now stays on screen through the opening animation instead of disappearing and coming back.
- **A clearer setting.** The Games row style setting now says what Steam and Spotlight do. If your row started to move
  differently after 3.3.0: `Prefer the selected game wide at the left? Quick Access → Game Glance → Spotlight Home → Games row style → Spotlight.`
